# Privacy Policy

**App:** Sudoku Everwood
**Developer:** Dvales tech
**Effective date:** October 13, 2026
**Contact:** dvalestech@gmail.com

## Summary
Sudoku Everwood is an offline Sudoku game. It does not collect, transmit or
share any personal information.

## Information we collect
None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
network connection. It works fully offline.

## Data stored on your device
Your game progress (levels, stars, times, statistics, coins and achievements)
and your settings (sound, music, vibration and music choice) are stored only
on your device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed
when you uninstall the app or clear its data, and you can erase progress at any
time from Settings > Reset Progress.

## Permissions
The app uses the vibration permission only, to provide haptic feedback. You can
turn this off in Settings.

## Third-party services
The app does not currently use any third-party services.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy
If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, by adding
ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Questions about this policy: dvalestech@gmail.com
