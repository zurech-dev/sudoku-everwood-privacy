# Privacy Policy

**App:** Sudoku Everwood
**Developer:** Dvales tech
**Effective date:** October 18, 2026
**Contact:** dvalestech@gmail.com

## Summary
Sudoku Everwood is a Sudoku game. The game itself works offline and does not
collect personal information. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which
is the only third party that receives data from the app.

## Information we collect
We do not collect your name, email, location or any account information. The
app has no accounts and no analytics.

## Data stored on your device
Your game progress (levels, stars, times, statistics, coins and achievements)
and your settings (sound, music, vibration and music choice) are stored only on
your device. This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the
app or clear its data, and you can erase progress at any time from
Settings > Reset Progress.

## Advertising
The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may
collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address,
device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Rewarded ads are
optional; you choose whether to watch them.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United
Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before ads are loaded, and you can change
your choice at any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Settings. You can also reset or
delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android
device settings.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions
- Internet and network state: used only to load and show ads.
- Vibration: used for haptic feedback; you can turn it off in Settings.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy
If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be
updated before the change is released, and the effective date above will change.
